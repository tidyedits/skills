# Grill my plan

Adapted from Matt Pocock's "grill-me" skill (mattpocock/skills), tuned for a Greater Boston cleaning business.

Interview me relentlessly about every part of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree and settle the decisions that others depend on first, one by one. If a question can be answered from project memory, project files or the website repo, look it up instead of asking.

## How
- Ask one question at a time, in plain words, and offer your best guess as a default answer so Yessi can reply "yes" from a phone.
- Cover, where they apply: who pays (realtors, property managers, landlords, Airbnb hosts, offices, homeowners), what they get, price and margin, how the lead arrives, who does the work and when, what it costs to run, what "working" looks like in numbers, and what would make us stop.
- Flag anything that spends money, signs Yessi up for something, or touches the live domain. Those need Yessi's yes.
- Stop when every branch is settled. Then give a short summary of the decisions and the first three actions.
