# No AI slop

Adapted from Peter Yang's "no-ai-slop" skill. Removes the tells that make copy sound machine-written, without sanding off the human parts.

## Remove
- "It's not X, it's Y" and other contrast setups.
- Openers like "Great question", "In today's fast-paced world", "Let's dive in", "Here's the thing", "What nobody tells you".
- Em dashes. Use a period or comma.
- Groups of three adjectives, stacked buzzwords ("seamless", "elevate", "unlock", "game-changer", "curated", "transform your space").
- Hype and fake urgency, rhetorical questions in a row, emoji walls, hashtag stuffing.
- Hedging filler ("it's worth noting", "in many cases") and summary lines that repeat the paragraph.

## Keep
- Specifics: the street, the job, the number, the detail we noticed (baseboards, grout, inside the oven).
- Short, uneven sentences and a plain, friendly tone. Tidy Edits sounds reliable, careful and easy to work with.
- Real contact details: 857-753-5593, info@tidyedits.com, tidyedits.com.

## Output
1. The cleaned copy.
2. A short list of each change and why, so Yessi can push back on any of them.
Never invent claims, reviews, prices or guarantees while rewriting.
