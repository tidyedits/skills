# Tidy triage

Adapted from Matt Pocock's "triage" skill, rebuilt for a cleaning business instead of bug reports.

## Sort every item into one bucket
1. **Today**: a paying or ready-to-book client waiting on us, a complaint, a job in the next 48 hours, a quote someone asked for.
2. **Quick wins**: under 10 minutes and moves money (reply to a warm lead, send a follow-up, confirm a booking).
3. **This week**: new outreach, recurring-client check-ins, marketing posts, website fixes.
4. **Later or drop**: nice ideas with no deadline, cold leads with no reply after 3 touches, spam.

## Rules
- Rank inside each bucket by value: recurring and commercial work first, then one-time large jobs (move-out, pre-sale, turnovers), then small one-offs.
- For each item give one line: who, what they want, the next action, and whether Claude can do it or it needs Yessi.
- Say plainly what Claude will do now without asking, and list only the items that need Yessi's own hands (calls, site visits, payments).
- Keep it short enough to read on a phone.
